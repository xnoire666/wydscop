# Wydscop privacy policy

Wydscop is a search bar that runs entirely on your PC.

- **What it reads:** the names, paths and dates of apps, files and folders on your PC, from the Start menu,
  the Windows Search index and its own index of folders Windows doesn't index. It does not read the
  contents of your files.
- **What it keeps:** your recent items, pinned results, how often you open each result, and your settings
  (theme, bar position and size). They are stored only on your PC, in your user profile.
- **What it sends:** nothing. Wydscop has no accounts, no network access, no analytics and no telemetry.
  No data leaves your PC.
- **Removing your data:** "Clear recent items" in the tray menu clears your recent items. Uninstalling
  Wydscop removes the rest.
