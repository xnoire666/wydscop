## Description

```
Wydscop is a fast search bar for Windows. Press Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters, and open any app, file or folder on your PC.

Apps, files and folders appear in one list, best match first. Results that match equally well are shown newest first, and every row shows where the item is and when it was last changed. Small typos are forgiven, so "chorme" still finds Chrome.

Look inside folders without leaving the bar: press → on a folder to see what's in it, and ← to go back to exactly where you were. The empty bar shows what you opened recently and the results you pinned.

Wydscop runs entirely on your PC. It has no accounts, no ads, no network access and no telemetry. It reads only the names, paths and dates of your apps and files, never their contents.
```
