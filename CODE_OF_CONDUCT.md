# Code of Conduct

Wydscop follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short:

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Respect different opinions and experiences. Disagree about code, not about people.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Focus on what's best for the project and the people using it.

## What's not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information (a real name, address or email) without their permission.
- Trolling, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In all of this project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when you represent
the project elsewhere.

## Reporting a problem

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request (the **⋯** menu), or report
the user from their profile (**Block or report**). You can also message the maintainer,
[xnoire666](https://github.com/xnoire666), on GitHub. Every report is read and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
